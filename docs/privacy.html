English

Privacy policy

Pegbar is a Photoshop plugin made by Coffee Cat Studio (Brazil). It does not collect, store or send any personal information.

  • The plugin has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
  • Everything it does happens inside Photoshop, on your computer: it reads and changes the layers of the document you have open.
  • Files are written only when you export (PNG or GIF), to the folder you choose.
  • Your settings (language, frame rate, onion skin and similar options) are saved only on your computer.
  • The plugin connects to the internet only when you click one of its links (website, user guide or Buy Me a Coffee). These open in your web browser, and those websites have their own privacy policies.
